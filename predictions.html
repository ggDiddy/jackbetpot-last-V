📚 Betting Glossary — Simple English

15 terms every beginner should know

1X2Match result: 1 = home win, X = draw, 2 = away win.
Asian HandicapA virtual head start given to one team (e.g., +1.5 goals) to balance the odds.
BTTSBoth Teams to Score — Yes/No bet.
Over/UnderBet on total goals or points being above or below a set line.
AccumulatorCombine multiple bets into one. All must win for payout.
BankrollThe total amount of money you set aside for betting.
Unit1–2% of your bankroll. Used to size bets consistently.
Value BetA bet where the odds are higher than the true probability suggests.
OddsThe payout multiplier. 2.00 = double your stake.
StakeThe amount you wager on a single bet.
Cash OutClose a bet early for a partial payout before the match ends.
Live BettingPlacing bets during a match as odds change in real time.
ParlaySame as accumulator — common term in US sports.
PushA tie where your stake is refunded (common in Asian handicaps).
BankerYour highest-confidence bet of the day.
